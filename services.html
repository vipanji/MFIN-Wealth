Services

Every stage of your money's journey, covered

From your first SIP to a fully managed portfolio approaching retirement — six services, one advisor who stays with you.

01 / SIP Planning

Systematic Investment Plans

Turn a fixed amount each month into a long-term habit, with fund selection matched to your goal and risk appetite.

  • Goal-based SIP calculators and projections
  • Fund selection across equity, hybrid and debt categories
  • Step-up SIPs as your income grows

02 / Tax-Saving (ELSS)

Equity-Linked Savings Schemes

Section 80C-eligible funds with the shortest lock-in among tax-saving instruments, chosen for long-term growth too.

  • Annual tax-planning review before the financial year closes
  • Fund comparison based on consistency, not just past returns
  • Lock-in and redemption planning

03 / Retirement Planning

Building your retirement corpus

A structured glide-path from growth-focused investing in your working years to capital protection as retirement nears.

  • Corpus target based on your desired retirement lifestyle
  • Mix of equity, debt and hybrid funds that shifts over time
  • Post-retirement income planning via SWP

04 / Portfolio Management

Ongoing portfolio review

Ongoing management and rebalancing so your existing investments stay aligned with your goals and market conditions.

  • Quarterly or half-yearly portfolio health checks
  • Rebalancing across asset classes and fund houses
  • Consolidated reporting across all your holdings

05 / Insurance Advisory

Protecting the plan you've built

Term and health insurance recommendations that sit alongside your investments, not compete with them.

  • Term cover sizing based on income and liabilities
  • Health insurance review for you and your family
  • Kept separate from investment products, by design

06 / Goal-Based Advisory

Planning around real milestones

One-on-one planning for the goals that don't fit a generic calculator — a child's education, a wedding, land, or business capital.

  • Timeline-based investment mapping per goal
  • Multiple concurrent goal tracking
  • Periodic goal-progress reviews
