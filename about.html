About us

A proprietorship built on patient, ethical investing

MFIN Wealth is an ARN-169435 registered mutual fund distribution and portfolio advisory proprietorship, based in India.

Our objective

Make disciplined investing accessible to every household

MFIN Wealth was set up to close a simple gap: Kerala has one of India's highest financial literacy rates, yet a large share of household savings still sits in gold, land and fixed deposits rather than instruments built for long-term growth. Our objective is to give local families, professionals, retirees and business owners a trusted, local point of contact for mutual fund investing — someone who explains the "why" behind every recommendation, not just the "what."

We operate as an AMFI-registered distributor under ARN-169435, and every recommendation follows SEBI-aligned, ethical advisory practices — full disclosure of costs, no mis-selling, and portfolios built around your goals rather than product targets.

Who we serve

How we work

Three commitments, every time

  1. Understand before recommending

    Every engagement starts with your goals, timeline and risk comfort — never a product pitch.

  2. Transparent, ethical advisory

    No black-hat sales tactics, no hidden costs. What you're recommended is on record and explained.

  3. Review, not "set and forget"

    Portfolios are revisited regularly and rebalanced as your goals or the market shift.
